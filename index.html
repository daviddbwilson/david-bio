David Wilson

I love making complex software feel simple.

I've been designing and building software since 9th grade, when my friend Ross and I talked a teacher into letting us mod a video game instead of doing our biology assignment. Neither of us could code, so it took months longer than the assignment would have! But we were hooked, and later founded two companies together:

  • Cape Networks, acquired by HPE Aruba. Network monitoring hardware and software: >$10M revenue, used by 2,000+ customers including Microsoft, Home Depot, and Disney.
  • Hunch: started as a data tool, became an AI workflow builder, then autonomous agents. One project reached 330K users in two weeks. We wound the company down in 2025.

Today I'm at Hex, where I work on our generative data apps and evals. I also hold the dubious distinction of being the company's top AI spender.

I'm from Cape Town and have lived on four continents. I now live in San Francisco with my family.
