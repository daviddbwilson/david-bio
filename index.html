David Wilson

Product builder & leader.

I started building software in 9th grade, modding video games before I could code, and later founded two companies:

  • Cape Networks, acquired by HPE Aruba. Network monitoring hardware and software: >$10M revenue, used by 2,000+ customers including Microsoft, Home Depot, and Disney. After the acquisition, I ran the business inside Aruba for a little under three years.
  • Hunch: started as a data tool, became an AI workflow builder, then autonomous agents. One project reached 330K users in two weeks. We wound down the company in late 2025.

I joined Hex in early 2026 to reimagine how we build dashboards. I took our generative data apps from first prototype to launch in two months, including building the evals. Since then I've worked on the last mile: the AI builds the app, but people still need precise control over the details. I also hold the somewhat dubious distinction of being the company's top AI spender.

I also work on personal projects. A fun recent one is Takeoff, a short interactive story. I wrote about how I made it.

I'm from Cape Town and have lived on four continents. I now live in San Francisco with my wife and two kids.
